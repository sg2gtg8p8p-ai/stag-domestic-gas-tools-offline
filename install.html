Stag Domestic Gas Tools

iPhone / iPad Home Screen installation

Browser testing has passed. Physical iPhone/iPad Airplane Mode testing is still pending. Install and test independently on each device before relying on it on site.

  1. While online, open the calculator directly in Safari at https://sg2gtg8p8p-ai.github.io/stag-domestic-gas-tools-offline/.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen. If available, enable Open as Web App. Tap Add.
  3. Open the new Stag Gas Tools Home Screen icon while still online. Wait for Offline copy verified • Ready to reopen without internet in that installed app. Do not assume Safari and the Home Screen app share an installed offline copy.
  4. Check the reference version displayed in the app. Optionally tap Keep offline copy; the operating system decides whether to grant persistent storage.
  5. Enable Airplane Mode and turn Wi-Fi off separately. Open each calculator, then close and reopen the Home Screen app with connectivity still disabled.

What to test

  • Gas rate: finish the selected timed test. For 100.000 → 100.100 m³ over 60 seconds, expect 6.00 m³/h, 64.83 gross kW and 58.41 net kW, matching the approved tool. Keep the timed test in the foreground.
  • Pipe sizing: compare default 30 kW gross / 5 m and routes with fittings against the approved ServiceM8 calculator.
  • Tightness: test the default U6 at 20 → 20 mbar, then 20 → 19 mbar. Confirm the unchanged retest requirement, pipework-only and smell-of-gas warnings.
  • Switch connectivity both ways. Confirm all three calculators still work and offline reopening succeeds. Inputs and results are intentionally not saved across closure.

Updates and recovery

Finish timed tests before accepting an update. A validated update waits for your explicit action and reloads that window, clearing unsaved inputs. A missing or corrupt offline package must be repaired online before use.

Reconnect and reopen, or tap Check / repair offline copy. If iOS has cleared website data, open the Home Screen app online and wait for the verified-ready message again. Do not clear unrelated websites or ServiceM8 data.

The offline app contains calculators only: no customer/job records, login, OAuth credentials or queued ServiceM8 writes. Copy result copies your calculation summary to the clipboard at your request. The existing ServiceM8 tool remains separate and unchanged.

Calculation aid only. All original warnings and controlled-standard/appliance-instruction checks remain applicable. Reference version: approved-20261009-622952a27038.
