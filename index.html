Natural Gas – Copper Pipe

Uses 38.9 MJ/m³ (10.80 kW per m³/h), relative density 0.6, copper efficiency 0.95 and Annex A formula methodology. Candidate bores: 13/19/25/32 mm for 15/22/28/35 mm copper.

Automatic Installation Volume

Pipe figures include the Edition 4 10% fittings allowance. For concealed pipework where size/length cannot be established, use the largest visible diameter for the estimated concealed run.

Test details

Edition 4 procedure reminders
Visual checks → ECV let-by at 7–10 mbar for 1 min → raise to relevant test pressure → 1 min stabilisation → adjust if necessary → 2 min test. Natural gas: where 20 mbar cannot be achieved, the procedure supplied for this tool allows a minimum 18 mbar test followed by the required operating-pressure checks. After a satisfactory test, reintroduce appliances and check disturbed joints/relevant isolated sections with LDF; there must be no smell of gas. Purge volume = 1.5 × IV.
